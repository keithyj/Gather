# Product copy

## Voice

Warm, direct, calm, and socially intelligent. Avoid corporate event-management language and avoid implying that verification guarantees safety.

## Key labels

- Create a gathering
- Request to join
- Approve guest
- Propose a plus-one
- Unlock details
- Event memories
- Add one of your three photos
- Private to attendees

## Trust reveal copy

**Before approval:** “The exact address is shared only after the host approves your place.”

**After approval:** “You’re on the guest list. The private event details are now unlocked.”

## Plus-one copy

“Know someone who would genuinely fit? Propose one person. The host will review the request before any private details are shared.”

## Photo capsule copy

“Choose up to three moments worth keeping. This album is visible only to approved attendees.”

## Safety copy

“Verification can reduce impersonation, but it does not guarantee someone’s identity, intentions, or safety. Trust your judgement and report concerns.”
