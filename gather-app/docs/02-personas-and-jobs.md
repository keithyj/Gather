# Personas and jobs to be done

## The connector host

Organises dinners, housewarmings, drinks, birthdays, and informal gatherings across multiple social circles. Values spontaneity but worries about capacity, safety, awkward introductions, and losing control of their address.

**Job:** “Help me bring compatible people together without turning planning into administration.”

## The invited friend

Receives too many fragmented invitations and may not know the other attendees. Wants enough context to decide without exposing personal data or downloading another app immediately.

**Job:** “Help me quickly understand whether this event is for me, respond, and know what happens next.”

## The bridge guest

Knows someone who would fit the event but does not want to overstep by forwarding a private address or assuming permission.

**Job:** “Let me introduce a plus-one respectfully and give the host final control.”

## The cautious attendee

Is interested in meeting new people but wants clarity about the host, venue type, conduct expectations, and who can see their profile and photos.

**Job:** “Give me confidence that this gathering is genuine and that I remain in control of my safety and privacy.”
