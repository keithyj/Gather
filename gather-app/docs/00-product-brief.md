# Product brief

## One-line proposition

Create trusted, intimate events across friendship groups, approve who joins, reveal sensitive details safely, and keep a private shared memory capsule afterwards.

## Problem

Small social events are currently organised through fragmented group chats, generic calendars, social networks, and one-off invitation links. Hosts struggle to invite across disconnected friendship groups, manage controlled plus-ones, protect a home address, track dietary information, and collect memories without exposing them publicly.

## Initial user

A socially active host organising a housewarming meal for people from several friendship groups.

## Core jobs

- Invite people without building another chaotic group chat.
- Let trusted guests introduce a plus-one without surrendering host control.
- Keep the exact venue hidden until attendance is approved.
- Make it easy for guests who do not yet have the app to respond.
- Create a shared photo record that is intimate rather than performative.

## Differentiated product loop

1. Host creates a private event with limited preview details.
2. Host invites across separate social circles.
3. Selected guests propose a plus-one with a short context note.
4. Host approves the attendee graph.
5. Approved guests unlock the exact location and event chat/updates.
6. During the event, each attendee contributes up to three photos.
7. After the event, attendees receive a private Polaroid memory capsule.
8. Guests can privately thank the host and signal what they enjoyed, creating useful host insights without public scoring.

## North-star outcome

Percentage of created private events that reach at least four approved attendees and produce a completed post-event memory capsule.

## MVP non-goals

- Becoming a public events marketplace.
- Replacing Google or Apple Calendar.
- Ticketing or payments.
- Public creator profiles.
- Public ratings, follower counts, or popularity scores.
- Dating or friend matching.
