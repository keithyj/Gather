# ADR 001: Private-first launch

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

Open events could provide growth and help people meet, but they materially increase risks involving home addresses, harassment, impersonation, age assurance, moderation, and emergency response.

## Decision

Launch only private, invite-led events for adults. Treat open-event discovery as a separate later product with stronger verification and operational safeguards.

## Consequences

- Lower initial growth surface.
- Faster and safer pilot.
- Cleaner value proposition around trusted introductions.
- Architecture must still avoid assumptions that every future event is private.
